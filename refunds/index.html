Refund Policy

Effective November 19, 2026. Plain English, no tricks.

The short version

If StatementFlip didn't work for you, email us and we will make it right or refund you. Write to support@senkarconsulting.com from the address you paid with.

What we refund

  • Single statement ($9): full refund within 14 days if we could not produce a usable file from your statement.
  • Subscriptions ($19 and $97 a month): full refund of your most recent charge if you ask within 14 days of it, and cancellation any time. We do not give partial refunds for unused days of a month you chose to keep.
  • Lifetime ($297): full refund within 30 days of purchase.
  • If we charge you by mistake or twice, we refund it in full.

How refunds are paid

We refund to the card or method you paid with, through Stripe. Your bank usually shows it in 5 to 10 business days. Stripe's processing fee on the original payment is not returned to us, but that never affects your refund.

After a refund

When a payment is refunded, the access it unlocked ends. A refunded single statement no longer unlocks.

Not working as expected?

Tell us which bank and statement type, and we will look into it. Fixing the converter for your statement is often better than a refund, but it is your choice. We never upload your file, so we cannot see it unless you choose to send it to us.
