Refund Policy

Effective November 19, 2026. Plain English, no tricks.

The short version

If StatementFlip didn't work for you, email us and we will make it right or refund you. Write to support@senkarconsulting.com from the address you paid with, and tell us what went wrong. We review every request in good faith and refund when the service did not do what we said it would. Unauthorized or duplicate charges are always refunded in full.

Subscriptions

You can cancel a subscription at any time by emailing us. Cancelling stops future charges, and your access continues to the end of the period you paid for.

How refunds are paid

We refund to the card or method you paid with, through Stripe. Your bank usually shows it in 5 to 10 business days.

After a refund

When a payment is refunded, the access it unlocked ends. A refunded single statement no longer unlocks.

Not working as expected?

Tell us which bank and statement type, and we will look into it. Fixing the converter for your statement is often better than a refund, but it is your choice. We never upload your file, so we cannot see it unless you choose to send it to us.
