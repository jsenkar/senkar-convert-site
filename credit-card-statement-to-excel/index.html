FAQ

Is it safe to convert my bank statement here?

Yes. Most converters upload your statement to their servers. Ours doesn't. The PDF is read by your own browser, so there's nothing for us to store or leak.

Does it work on scanned statements?

It works on digital PDFs downloaded from online banking (the vast majority). Photographed or scanned paper statements aren't supported yet.

Which banks are supported?

Most US banks and credit cards with downloadable PDF statements. If yours doesn't convert cleanly, email support@senkarconsulting.com and we'll add it, usually within a day.

Can I import the file into QuickBooks or Xero?

Yes. Pick the QuickBooks Online or Xero CSV in the download menu and use the bank-feed upload. The .QBO and OFX files are in beta.

Why not just copy-paste from the PDF?

Copy-paste scrambles columns, splits descriptions across lines, and loses negative signs. The converter keeps every transaction on one row with the right sign.

Do you offer refunds?

If a statement doesn't convert correctly, email us within 7 days for a full refund.
