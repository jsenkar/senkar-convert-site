Security and privacy

The short version: your statement is converted on your own computer. The file is not uploaded to us or anyone else.

What happens to your statement

  • The PDF is read by code running in your browser tab. While converting, your browser may fetch our own standard PDF-reading files (a worker script and fonts). Those are the same static files for everyone and contain nothing about your statement.
  • We never receive the file, its name, or any transactions. We have no server copy to lose, leak or hand over.
  • Closing the tab discards it. We do not keep your statements, and there are no accounts to log into.

Check it yourself

  1. Open this page, then open your browser's developer tools (F12, or right-click and choose Inspect) and go to the Network tab.
  2. Drop in a statement and convert it.
  3. Look at the Network tab. You will see only those standard files being downloaded from this site. You will see no upload and no request carrying your file or your transactions.

The only things that do leave your browser

  • Payment: checkout happens on Stripe's own page. Card details go to Stripe, not to us.
  • Unlocking after payment: when you return from Stripe, the page sends one thing to our server: the Stripe checkout session ID. Our server asks Stripe whether that session was paid and which plan it was, then replies. Subscribers' pages repeat this check about once a day. No statement data is part of it.
  • Campaign links: if you arrive from a link containing ?ref= (for example from an email from us), the page records that code, the time and the page you opened in a small log on our server, so we can tell which message brought a visit. It stores no cookie, no IP address and nothing about your statements. The code is also kept in your browser and passed to Stripe at checkout, so we can match a purchase to the message that led to it.
  • Ordinary web hosting: our host, like any website host, may log your IP address and the pages you request. We do not use advertising trackers.

What we cannot promise

Your access is saved in your own browser, so it is only as private as your own computer. Use a computer you trust, and avoid public ones. This page describes how the site works today. If that ever changes, we will say so here before it does. Questions? Email support@senkarconsulting.com.

FAQ

Is it safe to convert my bank statement here?

Yes. Most converters upload your statement to their servers. Ours doesn't. The PDF is read by your own browser, so there's nothing for us to store or leak.

Does it work on scanned statements?

It works on digital PDFs downloaded from online banking (the vast majority). Photographed or scanned paper statements aren't supported yet.

Which banks are supported?

Most US banks and credit cards with downloadable PDF statements. If yours doesn't convert cleanly, email support@senkarconsulting.com and we'll add it, usually within a day.

Can I import the file into QuickBooks or Xero?

Yes. Pick the QuickBooks Online or Xero CSV in the download menu and use the bank-feed upload. The .QBO and OFX files are in beta.

Why not just copy-paste from the PDF?

Copy-paste scrambles columns, splits descriptions across lines, and loses negative signs. The converter keeps every transaction on one row with the right sign.

Do you offer refunds?

If a statement doesn't convert correctly, email us within 7 days for a full refund.
