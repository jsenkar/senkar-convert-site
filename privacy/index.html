Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026. Plain English, no tricks.

Summary

StatementFlip is built so that we do not receive your bank statements. Conversion happens in your browser. This page explains the little information we do handle. The technical details are on our Security and privacy page.

What we do not collect

  • Your statements, their contents, or their file names. They never leave your browser.
  • Card numbers. Stripe collects those on its own checkout page.
  • Advertising or analytics trackers. We do not run any.

What we do handle

  • Payment information from Stripe: your email, name and billing details as entered at checkout, the plan you bought, and the payment and Stripe checkout session records. We use these to confirm your purchase, give you access, provide support, issue refunds and keep tax and accounting records.
  • Restoring access: if you ask us to restore access, the email you type is used once to look up your purchase at Stripe and to email you a link. We do not keep it from this step.
  • Access check: when you return from Stripe, and about once a day for subscribers, the site sends your Stripe checkout session ID to our server, which asks Stripe if it is paid and for which plan.
  • Campaign links: if you open a link containing ?ref=, we log that code, the time and the page path. No cookies, no IP address. The code is kept in your browser and passed to Stripe at checkout so we can tell which message led to a purchase.
  • Hosting logs: our web host (Hostinger) may log your IP address and the pages requested, as most web hosts do.
  • Support email: anything you send us at support@senkarconsulting.com.
  • In your browser: your plan and an optional campaign code are stored in your browser's local storage on your device.

Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information. Service providers handle it for us: Stripe (payments), Hostinger (website hosting and email). We may disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Payment and tax records are kept as long as the law requires. Support emails are kept as long as needed to help you and then deleted. The campaign log is kept while we run campaigns.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing support@senkarconsulting.com. We may need to keep payment records the law requires us to keep. You can clear your browser's site data at any time to remove the access and campaign code stored on your device. You will then need to ask us to restore access.

Children

StatementFlip is for adults and businesses and is not directed to children.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy we will update the date on this page. Questions: support@senkarconsulting.com, Senkar Consulting, LLC, 10251 West 44th Avenue, Unit 1-208, Wheat Ridge, Colorado 80033, USA.
